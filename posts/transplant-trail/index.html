BODEGA RUN

How To Fly

The Goal

Aim the cannon. Launch yourself across NYC — over an actual river — to unlock a borough's special item. Come down short and you splash: no unlock, though you keep whatever Borough Bucks you earned. There are four boroughs to collect. Keep flying as long as you want — exit to the trail whenever you're ready.

Aiming

Pick a borough to target first. Then a needle sweeps up and down through the launch angle — tap once to lock it in. A power meter swings next — tap again near the top to fire at full power.

Upgrades (buy in the shop after each turn)
  • Cannon Strength — how far you launch
  • Cannon Accuracy — slower angle sweep, easier to time
  • Flight Suit — tap to flap; better suit = more flaps
  • Rocket Power — one big mid-air boost
  • Special Bonus — coin magnet, bouncy legs, or rat whisperer
In The Air (good)
  • Coins — earn Borough Bucks
  • Rings — speed boost
  • Light clouds — bounce you up
  • Rainbow clouds — Happy Pride! (+bonus bucks)
  • Pretzels — restore flaps
  • Pigeon flock — free speed boost
  • Updrafts — strong lift
In The Air (bad)
  • Dark clouds — slow you way down
  • Pigeons (solo) — damage and knockback
  • Helicopters — heavy damage
On The Ground
  • Hit the ground fast → bounce → slide → stop
  • Dumpster — dead stop
  • Rat dumpster — enter rat mode (run along ground)
  • Fire hydrant — launches you back up
  • Subway grate — speed boost and liftoff
  • Hot dog cart — bounce
  • Manhole — trap, slows you
Borough Hazards (unique to each area)
  • Hoboken — pizza men launching discs skyward; drunks vomiting upward
  • Brooklyn Heights — new parents launching babies out of expensive strollers
  • Bushwick — vomiters
  • Astoria — Greek grandfathers smashing plates into the air (Opa!)
Borough Unlocks (land to collect)
  • Hoboken (350 blocks, across the Hudson) — Giant Cannoli + MAGA Girlfriend
  • Brooklyn Heights (650 blocks, across the East River) — Park Slope Food Co-op + Legal Summons
  • Bushwick (1300 blocks, across the East River) — Septum Piercing + ENM Marriage Reshuffle Goodybag
  • Astoria (1900 blocks, across the East River) — Reasonable Rent + Boring Personality

Act 2: The Heist

An old sugar factory on the Williamsburg waterfront

The Erics know a place. Brick the color of dried blood, arched windows four stories tall, and a floor that still smells faintly of sugar. Someone has pinned a store layout to a corkboard under the one working bulb.

The plan is simple in the way plans are simple before anyone runs them. You go in as the Thief. Everyone else needs a job.

Tony taps the corkboard like it owes him money. "You go in, you grab what's yours, we handle the rest. Simple." Ruhul is already redrawing his sketch of the floor plan for the third time. "Simple is doing a lot of work in that sentence." Dmitri lights a cigarette he won't smoke. "Everyone dies eventually," he says. "Tonight we also get money."

Caught

Four blocks from the bridge. Four blocks.

The car stops because the street stops. Red and blue on the brick, on the windshield, on all four of your faces. Somebody's hands go up first and then everybody's hands go up, which is how it always goes.

An officer counts the bag on the hood of the car, out loud, twice, because the number is somehow funnier the second time.

The take: $0

Tony is already loudly explaining to the nearest officer that this is a misunderstanding, that he is in fact very well-connected, that this whole business is beneath everyone involved. Ruhul just sighs and starts mentally re-costing the entire plan for next time. Dmitri looks almost relieved. "See," he says, to no one in particular. "I told you."
