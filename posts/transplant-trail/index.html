How To Fly

The Goal

Aim the cannon. Launch yourself across NYC. Land in a borough to unlock its special item. There are four boroughs to collect. Keep flying as long as you want — exit to the trail whenever you're ready.

Aiming

Pick a borough to target first. Then a needle sweeps up and down through the launch angle — tap once to lock it in. A power meter swings next — tap again near the top to fire at full power.

Upgrades (buy in the shop after each turn)
  • Cannon Strength — how far you launch
  • Cannon Accuracy — slower angle sweep, easier to time
  • Flight Suit — tap to flap; better suit = more flaps
  • Rocket Power — one big mid-air boost
  • Special Bonus — coin magnet, bouncy legs, or rat whisperer
In The Air (good)
  • Coins — earn Borough Bucks
  • Rings — speed boost
  • Light clouds — bounce you up
  • Rainbow clouds — Happy Pride! (+bonus bucks)
  • Pretzels — restore flaps
  • Pigeon flock — free speed boost
  • Updrafts — strong lift
In The Air (bad)
  • Dark clouds — slow you way down
  • Pigeons (solo) — damage and knockback
  • Helicopters — heavy damage
On The Ground
  • Hit the ground fast → bounce → slide → stop
  • Dumpster — dead stop
  • Rat dumpster — enter rat mode (run along ground)
  • Fire hydrant — launches you back up
  • Subway grate — speed boost and liftoff
  • Hot dog cart — bounce
  • Manhole — trap, slows you
Borough Hazards (unique to each area)
  • Hoboken — pizza men launching discs skyward; drunks vomiting upward
  • Brooklyn Heights — new parents launching babies out of expensive strollers
  • Bushwick — vomiters
  • Astoria — Greek grandfathers smashing plates into the air (Opa!)
Borough Unlocks (land to collect)
  • Hoboken (120 blocks) — Giant Cannoli + MAGA Girlfriend
  • Brooklyn Heights (300 blocks) — Park Slope Food Co-op + Legal Summons
  • Bushwick (550 blocks) — Septum Piercing + ENM Marriage Reshuffle Goodybag
  • Astoria (800 blocks) — Reasonable Rent + Boring Personality
